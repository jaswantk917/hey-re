Hey re is a <b>Hinglish voice assistant</b>. Say "lights band karo", "Maa ko message karo ki main aa raha hun" or "kitne steps hue aaj", in Hindi, English or both in one sentence, and it does it. It works out what you said and answers <b>on the phone itself</b>.

<b>What it does</b>
• <b>Check-in</b>: say hello for your steps, the Google Tasks due today, your notifications and a nudge to walk
• <b>Calls and messages</b> on WhatsApp, SMS and Slack, with a countdown to cancel
• <b>Google Home</b>: lights, plugs and the cooler, now or later
• <b>Phone switches</b> through Shizuku: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, airplane mode, Battery Saver, dark mode, location
• <b>Time, timers, alarms, sound mode, nearby places, notifications, weather, search, lyrics, translation, calculator, jokes, media, torch</b>

<b>Gemini Nano</b> (Pixel 9 and newer) understands requests the skills don't, asks before acting on its guesses, answers small questions offline and tells you what's on your screen.

Long-press the power button to summon it over any app or on the lock screen, where personal things wait for you to unlock.

Hey re is a fork of <a href="https://github.com/Stypox/dicio-android">Dicio</a>, licensed under the GNU GPL v3.
